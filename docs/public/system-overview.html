MerHouse System Overview

MerHouse coordinates fulfillment work between merchants and warehouse providers. The application keeps inventory, inbound stock, customer orders, shipment progress, exceptions, and service records aligned around an approved business relationship.

B2B fulfillment Merchant operations Warehouse workflows Service accountability

Connection Model

Merchant

  • Represents products and customer demand.
  • Plans inbound stock for a warehouse provider.
  • Reviews fulfillment progress and service records.
↔

Warehouse Provider

  • Receives stock into operational custody.
  • Handles pick, pack, ship, and exception work.
  • Records fulfillment evidence for shared review.

Workflow Story

1. Relationship

The merchant and warehouse provider operate through an approved fulfillment relationship.

2. Inbound

The merchant sends inbound stock plans; the warehouse provider receives and confirms work.

3. Fulfillment

Customer demand becomes warehouse work, then fulfillment progress becomes merchant-visible status.

4. Accountability

Shared records help both sides review what was agreed, what happened, and what needs attention.

Current Capabilities

Merchant Side

  • Create and update inventory items.
  • Submit inbound stock requests to connected warehouses.
  • Create customer orders and review fulfillment progress.
  • Review service agreements, statements, disputes, claims, and SLA status.

Warehouse Side

  • Receive inbound stock and maintain warehouse inventory.
  • Work allocation queues through pick, pack, ship, and delivery states.
  • Record packages, shipment evidence, and fulfillment exceptions.
  • Participate in service-accountability review workflows.
